# gaming-livestream-community-research
Bilingual exploratory user research on audience engagement, community identity and monetisation in gaming livestreams, using participant observation and comparative case studies.
# Gaming Livestream Communities
### Audience Engagement, Community Identity & Monetisation

**Exploratory User Research | Participant Observation | Comparative Case Study**

English | 中文

## 01. Research Overview
An exploratory comparative study of two gaming livestream communities, examining audience participation, community identity and monetisation practices.

## 02. Research Question
How do gaming categories, creator practices and community dynamics shape audience engagement and participation?

## 03. Methodology
- Participant observation
- Comparative analysis of two cases
- Qualitative interpretation of observed interactions

## 04. Key Research Themes
- Community roles and identity
- Audience engagement and retention
- Monetisation mechanisms and user motivations

## 05. Limitations
This exploratory study is based on two cases. Platform analytics and independently verified demographic data were unavailable. The findings do not establish causal relationships.

---

## 中文研究摘要

本研究通过对两个游戏直播社群的参与式观察与比较分析，探索游戏品类、主播互动方式与社群机制如何影响观众参与、身份认同及商业变现。

**研究方法：** 参与式观察、双案例比较、定性分析。

**研究局限：** 研究仅涉及两个案例，缺少平台后台数据及独立验证的人口统计资料，因此相关解释属于探索性发现，而非已证实的因果关系。
